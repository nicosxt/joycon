What is this?

A theme park made of people.

For one weekend, San Francisco turns into a playground of experiences that bring you back to your presence, your intuition, and maybe even your childhood self.

We call each experience a ride. Each ride is a recipe for connection. Some rides are built by us. Many are made by the people who come.

A letter from Mike and Cyan

Dear friend,

JoyCon is a festival that Cyan and I have been dreaming about for years. For a few days in January 2027, San Francisco becomes a playground of experiences that bring you back to your presence, your intuition, and maybe even into your childhood self.

We've prototyped dozens of experiences and are excited to share them with you: stonefruit omakases, Joy Safaris with no map and no phone, Magic Glasses dinners, even a Living Funeral. Each experience is a ride. Each ride is a recipe for connection. Some rides are ours. Many will be made by the people who come (you!).

JoyCon is an experiment. We're testing whether people are excited about a theme park for connection.

With joy and onwards,

Mike "Wonka" Wang
& Cyan "Poppins" Banister
